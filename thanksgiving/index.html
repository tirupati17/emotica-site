How it works

1

On your own phone. It is free to start.

2

Write their name and one line: "I'm grateful for you because..."

3

Send the link, or set it up together

Share it on WhatsApp or Messages. At the table on Thursday? Show the QR code and they scan it with their phone.

Our parents and grandparents rarely find an app like this on their own. But they will open a link from you that says thank you.

Emotica lets them talk instead of type, in English or Hindi, notice what lifts or weighs on their day, and wind down with breathing and sleep exercises. Everything they write stays private.
