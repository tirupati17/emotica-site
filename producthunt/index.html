Positioning

One-line pitch

Emotica turns a feeling into a small next step: a private space to notice what is here, then listen, write, or pause.

Who it is for

People who want a softer way to check in with themselves without a questionnaire, streak, or account wall.

Why now

Most mood tools ask for more data. Emotica starts with one honest feeling and gives it room.

Product Hunt listing

Suggested tagline

Suggested description

Try Emotica is a private space for how you feel. Name an emotion and watch it become part of your Mental Sky. Then choose what helps right now: a calming sound, a short guided exercise, or a few honest words. No questionnaire. No pressure. Just one small pause that can change the next minute.

Keep the listing concrete. Let the product do the explaining after the click.

Launch-day plan

  1. Before launch: test the landing page in an incognito window, verify the Product Hunt link, and prepare three short replies from real product observations.
  2. First hour: post the maker comment, reply to every genuine question, and invite people to try the web version before explaining features.
  3. Midday: share one product moment — a feeling becoming a cloud — with the direct link. Avoid asking for votes.
  4. Evening: collect repeated questions and publish a short update in the maker comment only if it adds useful context.
  5. After launch: thank participants, record friction points, and turn the best questions into the next product improvement.

Reply bank

“Is this therapy?”

No. Emotica is a reflection and listening tool, not medical care or a replacement for a professional.

“Do I need an account?”

No for the web experience. You can start with a feeling and see what happens.

“What should I try first?”

Pick the feeling that is closest, then follow the first suggestion. The point is a useful minute, not a perfect label.
