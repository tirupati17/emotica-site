Make room for how you feel.

Emotica brings mood tracking, a personal journal and calming exercises into one place. Start with a feeling, add its intensity, and see it become a cloud in your Mental Sky.

LOG A FEELING
Choose from feelings such as happy, calm, anxious or tired. Add thoughts and what brought the feeling on, or keep your check-in short.

SEE YOUR MENTAL SKY
Your logged feelings appear as clouds. Tap a cloud to revisit an entry, move it into history, and return to it later.

FIND AN EXERCISE
Get suggestions informed by your recent feelings. Explore guided meditations, sleep sounds, stories and other exercises, or choose something yourself. Round cards are sounds; rectangular cards open guided exercises and other audio content.

KEEP A JOURNAL
Write down thoughts in your own words. Keep your journal encrypted on your device, and use export and import to manage your backups. No account is needed to start.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose a theme, adjust the look of your cards, and decide whether to enable daily reminders. Notifications are optional.

START IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Onboarding and feeling labels are available in English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Spanish, Russian, German, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Some other screens and exercise titles use English.
